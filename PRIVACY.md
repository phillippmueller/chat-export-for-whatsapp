# Privacy statement

Chat Export for WhatsApp is a local tool. Its author collects no data from
you.

## What the tool processes

To export your chats, the tool reads WhatsApp's local data on your own
computer: on a Mac, a temporary copy of WhatsApp for macOS's chat and contacts
databases (your conversations, messages, contact names and phone numbers) and
the media files of the chats you export; for phone exports, only the files you
give it. It writes the export to a folder you choose on the same computer.
The temporary copy is deleted when the export finishes.

## What leaves your computer

Nothing from your WhatsApp data. The tool has no server, no account, no
telemetry and no analytics. If you use uv, uv downloads a Python runtime once
from its official source; that download carries no data about you or your
messages.

## When you use it with Claude

If you run the tool through Claude, Claude sees what the script prints
(chat names, counts and folder paths) in your conversation, like any other
command output. The skill instructs Claude not to open, read or quote message
content unless you ask for it. If you do ask Claude to analyse an export, or
attach a phone export to the conversation, that content becomes part of your
conversation and is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy and your Claude
settings, not by this tool.

## Your responsibility for exports

Exports are unencrypted copies of your private messages and of other
people's messages to you. You decide where they are stored and with whom they
are shared. Please respect the privacy of the people in your chats, who did not
agree to their messages being stored or processed elsewhere.
